## 1. What we collect

- **Account details.** Your name, email address, password (stored only as a secure hash by our authentication provider), the date you joined, and your settings, such as your language and the AI models you chose.
- **What you put into the Service.** Your apps and their details, competitor lists, uploads, brand files, prompts, creatives you generate, launch drafts and the tracking of ads you send to Meta, and the numbers you bring in for Measure.
- **Data from services you connect.** When you add a key for a provider, we fetch data from it on your behalf: for example ads and spend from your Meta ad account, subscription and revenue data from RevenueCat or Superwall, events from Mixpanel, attribution data from AppStack, and files from Google Drive. Research also collects ads and posts that competitors published publicly.
- **Your keys.** The API keys and tokens you add, stored encrypted, separately for each account. We keep only their last four characters in readable form, to show you which key is set.
- **Usage and technical data.** What you generate each month (to apply your plan's limits), request counts (to stop abuse), and the logs our hosting providers keep, such as IP addresses and error reports.

## 2. Why we use it

To run the Service for you (store your work, do what you ask, show your numbers); to keep accounts secure and apply plan limits; to answer you when you contact us; and to meet legal obligations. [List the legal basis for each use if GDPR or a similar law applies: contract, legitimate interests, consent, legal obligation.] We don't sell your personal data, and we don't use your content to train AI models.

## 3. Who we share it with

- **Hosting.** Supabase (database, file storage, sign-in and backend functions) and Vercel (the website). They process data only to provide their services to us.
- **Providers you connect.** When you use a feature that needs one, we send the data that feature needs to that provider, with your own key: OpenAI or TypeSafe (AI text, image reading and transcription), Apify (collecting public ads and posts), Higgsfield (images and videos), Meta (ad library searches, and creating paused ads and reading results in your ad account), RevenueCat, Superwall, Mixpanel, AppStack and Google Drive. Their own privacy policies apply to what they receive.
- **Sign-in protection.** If enabled, hCaptcha or Cloudflare Turnstile checks sign-up and sign-in forms. If you sign in with Google, Google shares your name and email with us.
- **When the law requires it**, or to protect people's rights and safety, and to a buyer if our business is sold (they must keep this policy's promises).

## 5. How long we keep it

We keep your data while your account exists. You can delete apps, creatives and stored files at any time, and deleting your account removes the rest (see section 7). Hosting providers' backups and logs may keep copies for up to [NUMBER] days before they are overwritten.

## 6. Cookies and browser storage

We use only what the Service needs to work. We use no advertising or tracking cookies.

- **Session cookies** keep you signed in.
- **Language cookie** remembers the language you picked.
- **Theme cookie** remembers light or dark mode.
- **Other preferences**, such as the app you opened last and tips you dismissed, and a copy of lists kept in your browser for faster loading. Signing out clears that copy.

## 7. Deleting your account

You can delete your account yourself in Settings › Account › Delete my account. This permanently deletes your apps, the ads and posts collected for them, your creatives, your launch drafts and tracking data, your Measure data, your stored files, your saved keys, and your sign-in. Ads already created in your own Meta ad account are not deleted: manage them in Meta Ads Manager. Data you sent to providers you connected is kept under their own policies. While the deletion runs your account is locked. Afterwards we keep your account's internal ID for up to 24 hours, only to remove files that arrive late, and the internal IDs of your deleted apps, so they are never reused; neither includes your name or email.

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, export or delete your personal data, to object to or restrict how we use it, and to complain to a data protection authority. Most of this you can do in the Service; for anything else write to {{contact}}. We will answer within the time the law requires.

## 9. Security

Your keys are stored encrypted, each account's data is kept separate from others', and access to our systems is limited. No service can be perfectly secure; if a breach affects your data we will tell you as the law requires.

## 10. Children

The Service is not for anyone under 18, and we don't knowingly collect their data.
